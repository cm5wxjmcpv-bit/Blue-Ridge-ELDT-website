Common questions

Can I take the course completely online?

Yes. This is the ELDT theory portion for the Hazmat endorsement and is completed online.

Do I get the H endorsement when I finish?

No. Passing this course satisfies the applicable ELDT theory training requirement. You still complete the remaining State and TSA requirements for the endorsement.

What score do I need?

You must score at least 80% on the final knowledge assessment.

What information do I need to enroll?

Your legal name, date of birth, driver license/CLP/CDL number, issuing State, email address, phone number, and a username and password for your training account.
